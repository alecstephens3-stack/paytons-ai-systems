# Decision Log

## [Decision name]

- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Decision:** [choice]
- **Reason:** [short explanation tied to evidence and constraints]
- **Tradeoff accepted:** [what the choice gives up]
- **Confidence:** [low, medium, or high, with reason]
- **Owner:** [person responsible]
- **Outcome and horizon:** [change sought and when it should be observable]
- **Baseline:** [value or current state, observation date, source, population and measurement window; unknown if unavailable]
- **Target and leading indicator:** [success condition; early signal and why it should predict the outcome]
- **Guardrail:** [quality, risk, cost or other outcome that must not deteriorate]
- **Prediction and limits:** [range, controlling assumption, acceptable downside and evidence that would change the decision]
- **Evidence:** [brief, comparison, source, or test]
- **Revisit when:** [observable condition]
- **Review owner and date:** [who checks the result and when; a date alone is not a scheduled job]
- **Continue / change / stop:** [conditions chosen before the outcome]
- **Status:** [active, superseded, or under review]

Use the fields that affect the decision. Keep missing evidence visible, and preserve the original record when the result arrives.
